Food Delivery Operations and Gross Order Value: During the fiscal reporting year, our core food delivery business sustained robust expansion in Gross Order Value (GOV), propelled by expanding transacting user frequency and consistent geographic expansion across tier-2 and tier-3 Indian cities. Adjusted EBITDA for the food delivery segment achieved meaningful positive profitability, reflecting route density gains, restaurant partner commission rationalization, and the introduction of nominal platform convenience fees. Dining-out and live events services experienced renewed consumer engagement, providing synergistic advertising channels for premier culinary brands and hospitality partners.

[FILING-CHUNK-2]
Quick Commerce (Blinkit) Hypergrowth and Dark Store Economics: Quick commerce continues to represent our fastest-scaling operational engine, with Blinkit posting triple-digit YoY GOV growth. The network expanded its micro-fulfillment dark store footprint aggressively across key metropolitan clusters while simultaneously improving average store-level contribution margins toward break-even. Increasing product assortment breadth—extending beyond impulse groceries into consumer electronics, beauty, toys, and festive merchandise—has expanded average basket order values, enhancing fulfillment labor and delivery fleet asset productivity.

[FILING-CHUNK-3]
Hyperpure B2B Supply Chain and Working Capital Profile: The Hyperpure vertical, our farm-to-fork wholesale supply business catering directly to restaurant partners, witnessed rapid volume scale. While Hyperpure operates on lower gross product margins compared to consumer marketplace services, it creates sticky supply chain integration across the dining ecosystem. The consolidated working capital profile benefits from negative net working capital dynamics inherent in upfront customer digital prepayments paired with standard 15-to-30 day trade payable settlements with vendor aggregators. Capital expenditure remained strictly asset-light, focusing on automated sorting hubs and temperature-controlled dark store racking.

[FILING-CHUNK-4]
Monetization Levers, Take Rates, and Zomato Gold: Consolidated take-rate economics showed consistent positive momentum driven by restaurant ad-tech bidding platforms, branded sponsored placement auctions, and higher Gold membership subscription renewals. Zomato Gold members demonstrated 2.8x higher ordering cadence compared to non-members, significantly driving customer lifetime value (LTV) despite offering priority delivery and dining discounts. Segment marketing expenditure as a percentage of GOV reduced sustainably as customer organic acquisition and brand recall solidified.

[FILING-CHUNK-5]
Intense Competitive Rivalry and Regulatory Headwinds: The company operates in a hyper-competitive Indian consumer internet and quick commerce market contested by aggressive well-capitalized incumbents such as Swiggy and Zepto. Intense local competition for delivery rider capacity, geographic store density, and merchant exclusivity covenants can precipitate promotional discounting wars, adversely affecting contribution margins. Additionally, emerging regulatory scrutiny regarding gig-worker social security safety nets, gig platform welfare cess proposals, and minimum payout frameworks could increase delivery delivery unit labor costs over the medium term.

[FILING-CHUNK-6]
Capital Reserves, Treasury Liquidity, and Profitability Trajectory: Our fortress cash reserves, exceeding INR 10,000 Crores held primarily in short-term sovereign treasury bills and bank fixed deposits, furnish complete operational autonomy without dependence on external debt markets. The company maintains an uncompromised zero long-term debt profile. Management remains steadfast on its strategic roadmap toward realizing a consolidated 4% to 5% adjusted EBITDA margin profile on total GOV over the medium-term horizon through operating leverage and quick commerce profitability scaling.
